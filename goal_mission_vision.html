Our Purpose

Building awareness, encouraging preparedness, and creating safer communities.

Our Vision

To create a safer and more resilient Myanmar where individuals and communities are knowledgeable, prepared, and capable of responding effectively to earthquakes.

Our Mission

To provide accessible and reliable earthquake information, practical safety guidance, and educational resources that help people understand earthquake risks and take action to protect themselves and others.

Our Goal

To increase earthquake awareness in Myanmar by sharing historical events, real-life data, safety regulations, and preparedness strategies while encouraging community participation and disaster risk reduction.
