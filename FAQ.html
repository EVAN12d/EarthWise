Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about earthquakes, their impacts, and how to stay prepared.

Understanding Earthquakes

What is an earthquake?

An earthquake is the sudden shaking of the Earth's surface caused by the release of energy within the Earth's crust. This usually happens when rocks suddenly move along faults.

What causes earthquakes?

Most earthquakes are caused by tectonic plate movements. Other causes include volcanic activity and human activities such as mining, large reservoirs, and underground fluid injection.

Why is Myanmar vulnerable to earthquakes?

Myanmar is located in a geologically active region where tectonic plates interact. It also has several active faults, including the Sagaing, Kyaukkyan, and Kabaw Faults.

Which areas in Myanmar are at higher risk?

Areas near major fault lines face greater earthquake risks. These include Mandalay, Sagaing, Bago, Taungoo, Naypyidaw, parts of Shan State, and areas near the Indo-Burman Ranges.

Earthquake Impacts

What are the possible impacts of earthquakes?

Earthquakes can damage buildings, roads, bridges, schools, and hospitals. They can also cause injuries, casualties, displacement, and disruption to essential services.

Can earthquakes cause other natural disasters?

Yes. Earthquakes can trigger secondary hazards such as landslides, tsunamis, soil liquefaction, and fires. These hazards may cause additional damage to communities.

What happened during the 2025 Myanmar earthquake?

On 28 March 2025, a magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck central Myanmar near Sagaing. It caused widespread damage across several regions, affecting homes, schools, hospitals, and communities.

Can scientists predict when an earthquake will happen?

No. Scientists cannot accurately predict the exact date, time, location, or magnitude of an earthquake. However, they can identify earthquake-prone areas and estimate long-term seismic risks.

Safety and Preparedness

What should I do during an earthquake?

Follow the Drop, Cover, and Hold On procedure. Drop onto your hands and knees, cover your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk, and hold on until the shaking stops.

What should I prepare before an earthquake?

Prepare an emergency kit containing drinking water, non-perishable food, a flashlight, a first-aid kit, essential medicines, and other necessary supplies. You should also secure heavy furniture and practise an emergency communication plan.

What should I do after an earthquake?

Check yourself and others for injuries, expect aftershocks, and stay away from damaged buildings and fallen power lines. Follow reliable emergency updates and leave unsafe buildings when it is safe to do so.

Is there a way to prevent earthquakes?

Earthquakes are natural geological events that humans cannot prevent. However, we can reduce their impacts through earthquake-resistant buildings, safety education, emergency planning, and community preparedness.

Community and Resources

How can communities reduce earthquake risks?

Communities can improve building safety, participate in earthquake drills, secure public facilities, spread awareness, and prepare emergency resources before disasters occur.

How can I help earthquake-affected communities?

You can support affected communities through trusted charity organisations, responsible donations, awareness campaigns, and appropriate volunteer activities.

Where can I find reliable earthquake information?

You can find earthquake information through organisations such as the United States Geological Survey (USGS), UNICEF, the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC), and relevant Myanmar authorities.

What is the purpose of this website?

Our website aims to raise awareness about earthquake hazards in Myanmar, explain their causes and impacts, and provide practical safety guidelines and preparedness information to help communities reduce risks.

Stay informed. Stay prepared.

Understanding earthquake risks and taking action today can help protect communities tomorrow.
