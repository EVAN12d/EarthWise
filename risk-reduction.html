Earthquake Risk Zones in Myanmar

Myanmar is affected by the Sagaing Fault, which runs from north to south. Areas near the fault tend to experience stronger shaking and higher earthquake risks.

Sagaing Fault

The Sagaing Fault is a major active fault that runs through Myanmar. Earthquakes can occur along this fault, affecting nearby regions such as Mandalay, Sagaing, Bago and Yangon.

Important Facts!

Areas near fault lines and on soft soil can face a higher risk of strong shaking and greater damage.

Why Do Earthquakes Cause Damage?

The level of damage depends on three main factors. Understanding these factors helps us find better ways to reduce risk.

Hazard

The strength and location of an earthquake and ground conditions such as soft soil.

Exposure

The number of people, homes and buildings located in earthquake-prone areas.

Vulnerability

How easily buildings and communities can be damaged by an earthquake.

Vulnerable Building
  • Unreinforced bricks
  • Weak structure
  • More likely to collapse
  • Higher risk of injury
Earthquake-Resistant Building
  • Strong structural design
  • Uses reinforced materials
  • More earthquake-resistant
  • Safer for people inside

How Can We Reduce The Risk?

Government, engineers, and communities can work together to reduce earthquake risks through planning, stronger construction and better infrastructure.

Building Codes

Follow and enforce earthquake-resistant design standards.

Retrofitting Existing Buildings

Strengthen older buildings to make them more resistant.

Land-Use Planning

Avoid high-risk areas and plan safer locations for homes and schools.

Infrastructure Protection

Make sure important facilities such as bridges and hospitals are more resilient.

A Safer Myanmar is Possible

Through stronger buildings, smarter planning and a moreprepared community, we can reduce earthquake risks and protect lives.
