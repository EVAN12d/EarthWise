Stay Protected.

Learn essential safety steps to protect yourself, your family, and your community before, during, and after an earthquake.

Safety Starts With You

Small steps today can make a big difference when an earthquake happens.

Learn. Prepare. Stay Safe.
SAFETY GUIDES

Explore Safety Guides

Learn what to do before, during, and after an earthquake. Explore additional guidance using the arrows.

BEFORE

Prepare in Advance

Prepare your home, emergency supplies, and family safety plan before an earthquake.

Before an Earthquake
  • Prepare an emergency kit with food, water, and a flashlight.
  • Secure heavy furniture and objects that could fall.
  • Identify safe places away from windows.
  • Create a family emergency communication plan.
  • Practise earthquake drills regularly.
DURING

Stay Safe

Learn how to protect yourself from falling objects and dangerous shaking.

During an Earthquake
  • Drop onto your hands and knees.
  • Cover your head and neck under a sturdy table if nearby.
  • Hold on until shaking stops.
  • Stay inside if you are indoors.
  • Stay away from windows and falling objects.
AFTER

Recover and Stay Alert

Check for danger, prepare for aftershocks, and follow reliable safety updates.

After an Earthquake
  • Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Expect aftershocks.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and power lines.
  • Follow official emergency information.
  • Contact family members when safe.
BEFORE

Structural & Home Safety

Make your living space safer by identifying and reducing possible hazards.

Home Safety Tips
  • Secure bookcases and wardrobes.
  • Keep heavy objects on lower shelves.
  • Keep exits and walkways clear.
  • Use safety latches on cabinets.
  • Ask professionals to inspect structural concerns.
DURING

Navigating Public Spaces

Learn how to stay safe in crowded places or unfamiliar locations.

Public Space Safety
  • Protect your head and neck during shaking.
  • Stay away from shelves and falling objects.
  • Do not rush toward crowded exits.
  • Avoid elevators.
  • Near the coast, move to higher ground after strong or prolonged shaking.
AFTER

First Response & Logistics

Understand important safety steps to take after an earthquake.

First Response
  • Check for injuries and request medical help if needed.
  • Provide first aid only within your training.
  • Avoid damaged electrical equipment.
  • Wear sturdy shoes around debris.
  • Keep roads clear for emergency responders.
SAFETY GUIDE

Immediate Earthquake Response

Remember these three simple steps to protect yourself during an earthquake.

DROP

Down onto your hands and knees before the earthquake knocks you down.

COVER

Your head and neck (and your entire body if possible) underneath a sturdy table or desk.

HOLD ON

To your shelter until the shaking stops. Be prepared to move with your shelter if it shifts.

LOCATION-BASED SAFETY

Safety in Different Locations

Learn how to stay safe in different environments during an earthquake.

Indoors & High-Rise

  • Stay indoors during shaking.
  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Follow building safety instructions afterward.

Stadiums & Theaters

  • Stay in your seat during shaking.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Do not rush toward exits.
  • Follow staff instructions afterward.
  • Leave carefully when safe.

Outdoor Areas

  • Stay outdoors if already outside.
  • Move away from buildings when safe.
  • Avoid trees and power lines.
  • Get low and protect your head.
  • Watch for damaged structures afterward.

Moving Vehicles

  • Pull over safely.
  • Avoid bridges and power lines.
  • Stay inside until shaking stops.
  • Listen for emergency updates.
  • Watch for damaged roads afterward.
